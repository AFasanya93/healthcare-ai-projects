# 30-60-90 Day Plan — PV-Copilot

## Day 0-30: Foundation & Validation Design
**Goal:** Environment ready, model configured on historical data, validation plan approved.

| Week | Milestone | Owner | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kickoff; finalize vendor SOW & data handling agreement | Dana Whitfield (PM) | Signed SOW, DPA |
| 1 | Stand up sandboxed environment, isolated from Argus prod | IT Security Lead | Environment access confirmed |
| 2 | De-identify & extract 24 months of historical Meridian-X cases | PV Data Analyst | De-identified dataset (n≈8,000 cases) |
| 2-3 | Configure AI model on historical cases; initial calibration | Vendor SE + PV Coder SME | Calibrated model v0.1 |
| 3 | Draft validation protocol (IQ/OQ-lite for advisory tool) | QA Validation Lead | Draft validation protocol |
| 4 | Steering Committee review of validation protocol & pilot readiness | Dana Whitfield | Approved protocol; go/no-go for pilot start |

**Exit criteria for Day 30:** Environment live, model calibrated on historical data, validation protocol signed off, pilot case volume agreed (target 150 cases/week).

## Day 31-60: Live Pilot & Parallel Review
**Goal:** Run the copilot against live incoming Meridian-X cases in shadow/advisory mode; measure concordance and cycle time.

| Week | Milestone | Owner | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Go-live: AI pre-triages live cases; PV physicians review 100% | Dana Whitfield | Pilot launch confirmation |
| 5-6 | Weekly concordance measurement begins (AI vs. final human code) | PV Coder SME | Weekly concordance report |
| 6 | Mid-pilot checkpoint: cycle time & concordance trend review | Steering Committee | Checkpoint memo |
| 7 | Tune model thresholds based on Week 5-6 misses | Vendor SE | Model v0.2 |
| 8 | Signal-flagging dashboard UAT with PV physicians | PV Physicians | UAT sign-off |

**Exit criteria for Day 60:** ≥2 consecutive weeks of concordance data collected, cycle time trending toward ≤1.5 days, no unresolved critical issues in RAID log.

## Day 61-90: Stabilize, Validate, Recommend
**Goal:** Hit target KPIs, complete validation package, deliver go/no-go for broader rollout.

| Week | Milestone | Owner | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Confirm ≥90% concordance sustained 2 consecutive weeks | PV Coder SME | KPI attainment report |
| 10 | Compile validation evidence package (protocol + results + deviations) | QA Validation Lead | Validation package draft |
| 11 | Draft rollout business case (cost, scope options, timeline) for scale-up | Dana Whitfield | Business case v1 |
| 12 | Safety Committee go/no-go decision meeting | Dr. Elena Kowalski (sponsor) | Go/no-go decision + next-phase charter (if go) |
| 12 | Project retrospective & lessons learned | Dana Whitfield | Retro notes, filed with PMO |

**Exit criteria for Day 90:** KPIs met or documented gap analysis provided, validation package complete, formal go/no-go decision recorded.
