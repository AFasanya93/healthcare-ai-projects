# Communications Plan — PV-Copilot

| Audience | Content | Channel | Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steering Committee | Progress vs. plan, KPI trend, risks/decisions needed | Live meeting + 1-page memo | Biweekly | Dana Whitfield |
| Working Team | Task status, blockers, upcoming work | Standup | Weekly (async doc + 15-min sync) | Dana Whitfield |
| PV Physicians (reviewers) | Concordance results, model tuning changes affecting their workflow | Email + working session | Weekly during pilot (Day 31-90) | PV Coder SME |
| Safety Committee | Go/no-go recommendation & evidence package | Formal presentation | Once, Day ~85-90 | Dana Whitfield + Dr. Kowalski |
| Broader PV Department | Awareness that a pilot is underway, no workflow change expected outside pilot cohort | Email announcement | Once, at Day 30 go-live | Dr. Elena Kowalski |
| QA/Regulatory | Validation protocol status, deviations log | Shared tracker + biweekly steering | Continuous | QA Validation Lead |

## Escalation Path
1. Working-team-level blocker → raised at weekly standup, owner assigned 24-48h SLA.
2. Unresolved after 48h or cross-functional → escalated to Steering Committee (or ad hoc call if urgent).
3. Safety-critical issue (e.g., suspected missed signal) → immediate notification to Dr. Kowalski and QA Director within 4 hours, independent of standing cadence.
