# Communications Plan — ScribeOS

| Audience | Content | Channel | Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steering Committee | KPI trend, security/consent audit status, risks/decisions | Live meeting + 1-page memo | Biweekly | Marcus Ilić |
| Working Team | Task status, blockers | Standup | Weekly | Marcus Ilić |
| Pilot Physicians/APPs | Tool tips, feedback loop closure, quality audit results | Team huddle + email | Weekly during pilot | Physician Champions |
| Non-pilot physicians (system-wide) | Awareness that a pilot is underway; what to expect if it scales | Email announcement | Once, at Day 30 go-live | Dr. Anthony Reyes |

| Compliance/Privacy | Consent-compliance audit results, retention verification | Shared tracker + biweekly steering | Continuous | Compliance/Privacy Officer |
| Clinical Informatics Steering Committee | Scaling recommendation & evidence package | Formal presentation | Once, Day ~85-90 | Marcus Ilić + Dr. Reyes |

## Escalation Path
1. Workflow friction / minor tool issue → raised at weekly huddle, Champion triages within 48h.
2. Integration/technical blocker → escalated to IT/Epic Integration Analyst and Vendor Lead same day.
3. Suspected PHI retention violation or consent non-compliance → immediate notification to CISO and Compliance Officer within 4 hours; pilot pauses at affected clinic pending review if violation confirmed.
