# 30-60-90 Day Plan — ScribeOS

## Day 0-30: Security Review, Integration & Consent Design
**Goal:** Security/privacy risk assessment passed, Epic integration built, consent workflow designed and approved.

| Week | Milestone | Owner | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kickoff; finalize vendor contract, BAA, data retention terms | Marcus Ilić (PM) | Signed contract, BAA |
| 1-2 | Security/privacy risk assessment (data flow, retention, encryption review) | CISO / Privacy Analyst | Risk assessment report |
| 2 | Design patient verbal-consent workflow (script, documentation method, opt-out path) | Physician Champions + Compliance | Approved consent workflow |
| 2-3 | Build & test Epic ambulatory notes integration in non-prod environment | IT/Epic Integration Analyst | Integration test results |
| 3 | Physician recruitment for pilot cohort (target 25+ of 35 eligible) | Clinic Medical Directors | Confirmed opt-in roster |
| 4 | Steering Committee review: security sign-off, consent workflow approval, go/no-go for go-live | Marcus Ilić | Go/no-go decision |

**Exit criteria for Day 30:** Security assessment passed (or documented remediations complete), consent workflow approved by Compliance, Epic integration tested, ≥25 physicians opted in.

## Day 31-60: Go-Live & Early Adoption Monitoring
**Goal:** Tool live across 3 clinics, documentation-time and note-quality baselines established.

| Week | Milestone | Owner | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Go-live at Clinic 1 (staggered rollout) | Physician Champion (Clinic 1) | Go-live confirmation |
| 5-6 | Go-live at Clinics 2 and 3 | Physician Champions | Go-live confirmation |
| 6 | Week 1-2 documentation-time measurement vs. matched control clinic | IT Analytics | Weekly time-tracking dashboard |
| 7 | First chart-quality audit sample (n=100 notes across 3 clinics) | Clinic Medical Directors | Audit results memo |
| 8 | Physician feedback session — workflow friction, edit burden, trust in AI drafts | Physician Champions | Feedback summary + action items |

**Exit criteria for Day 60:** All 3 clinics live ≥3 weeks, ≥90% consent-workflow compliance in audit sample, no critical documentation errors identified.

| Week | Milestone | Owner | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Confirm documentation-time reduction ≥40% sustained 2+ weeks | IT Analytics | KPI attainment report |
| 10 | Second chart-quality + consent-compliance audit | Clinic Medical Directors + Compliance | Final audit report |
| 11 | Draft system-wide scaling business case (cost, phased rollout plan, staffing) | Marcus Ilić | Business case v1 |
| 12 | Clinical Informatics Steering Committee go/no-go decision | Dr. Anthony Reyes (sponsor) | Go/no-go decision + next-phase charter (if go) |
| 12 | Project retrospective | Marcus Ilić | Retro notes filed with PMO |

**Exit criteria for Day 90:** Documentation-time and note-quality KPIs met or gap analysis documented, consent/security audits closed, formal scaling decision recorded.
