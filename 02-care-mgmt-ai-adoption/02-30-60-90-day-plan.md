# 30-60-90 Day Plan — RiskLens

## Day 0-30: Setup, Integration & Fairness Baseline
**Goal:** Data feeds connected, model configured, fairness baseline established, control pod selected.

| Week | Milestone | Owner | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kickoff; finalize vendor contract, BAA, data-processing scope | Priya Nandakumar (PM) | Signed contract, BAA |
| 1-2 | Connect claims/pharmacy/CM-notes feeds to vendor platform | IT Integration Engineer | Feed connectivity confirmed |
| 2 | Select 2 pilot pods + 1 matched control pod (similar acuity mix) | Data/Analytics Lead | Pod assignment memo |
| 3 | Initial model run on historical 12-month data; baseline risk scores generated | Vendor Implementation Lead | Baseline model output |
| 3 | Fairness/bias baseline review (scoring parity across demographic groups) | External Statistician | Baseline fairness report |
| 4 | Steering Committee review: integration status, baseline fairness results, go/no-go for training | Priya Nandakumar | Go/no-go decision |

**Exit criteria for Day 30:** Data feeds live, baseline fairness report shows no material disparity flags (or documented mitigation plan if flags found), pilot/control pods confirmed.

## Day 31-60: Training, Go-Live & Early Monitoring
**Goal:** Care managers trained, tool live in daily workflow, early KPI trend established.

| Week | Milestone | Owner | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Care manager training (half-day sessions, both pilot pods) | Care Manager Champions | Training completion log |
| 5 | Go-live: daily risk scores + next-best-action recommendations live in CM platform | Priya Nandakumar | Go-live confirmation |
| 6 | Week 1 KPI check: time-to-outreach, gap closure rate (pilot vs. control) | Data/Analytics Lead | Week 1 KPI dashboard |
| 7 | Care manager feedback session — usability, recommendation relevance | Care Manager Champions | Feedback summary + action items |
| 8 | Mid-pilot fairness re-check on live scoring data | External Statistician | Mid-pilot fairness report |

**Exit criteria for Day 60:** ≥3 weeks of live KPI data collected, no unresolved fairness flags, care manager adoption rate ≥80% (logging into and acting on daily recommendations).

## Day 61-90: Optimize, Validate Impact, Recommend
**Goal:** Confirm KPI improvement vs. control, finalize fairness sign-off, deliver expansion recommendation.

| Week | Milestone | Owner | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Confirm time-to-outreach and gap-closure improvement vs. control pod | Data/Analytics Lead | Impact analysis report |
| 10 | Final fairness/bias audit and sign-off | External Statistician | Final fairness audit report |
| 11 | Draft expansion business case (remaining 6 pods, staffing/budget implications) | Priya Nandakumar | Business case v1 |
| 12 | Value-Based Care Steering Committee go/no-go decision | Renee Ostrowski (sponsor) | Go/no-go decision + next-phase charter (if go) |
| 12 | Project retrospective | Priya Nandakumar | Retro notes filed with PMO |

**Exit criteria for Day 90:** Statistically meaningful KPI comparison vs. control documented, fairness audit closed out, formal expansion decision recorded.
