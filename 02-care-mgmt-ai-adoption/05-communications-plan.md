# Communications Plan — RiskLens

| Audience | Content | Channel | Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steering Committee | KPI trend vs. control, fairness audit status, risks/decisions | Live meeting + 1-page memo | Biweekly | Priya Nandakumar |
| Working Team | Task status, blockers | Standup | Weekly | Priya Nandakumar |
| Care Managers (pilot pods) | Tool usage tips, recommendation-tuning updates | Team huddle + email | Weekly during pilot | Care Manager Champions |
| Control Pod Supervisor | Reminder of isolation protocol, no tool access | Email | Once at kickoff + reminder at Day 45 | Priya Nandakumar |
| Compliance/Legal | BAA status, data scope adherence, fairness audit results | Shared tracker + as-needed | Continuous, formal review at Day 1 and Day 70 | Hector Delgado |
| Value-Based Care Steering Committee | Expansion recommendation & evidence package | Formal presentation | Once, Day ~85-90 | Priya Nandakumar + Renee Ostrowski |

## Escalation Path
1. Pod-level issue (usability, workflow friction) → raised at weekly huddle, Champion triages within 48h.
2. Data/technical blocker → escalated to Vendor Implementation Lead + IT Integration Engineer same day.
3. Fairness/bias flag of any severity → immediate notification to Compliance Officer and Steering Committee within 24 hours, independent of standing cadence; go-live/continuation paused pending review if flag is material.
